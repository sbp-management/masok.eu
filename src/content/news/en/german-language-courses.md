MASOK Academy's language courses are open to anyone interested – in German and many other languages, including English, Spanish, Italian, French, Danish, Russian and Serbian for foreigners. Classes are taught according to German quality standards, in small groups or one-to-one, by experienced instructors.

## Levels and formats

Courses start at beginner level (A1) according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). German goes up to B2; other languages and levels are arranged individually. A free placement test puts you in the right group. Formats include general language courses, professional language for medicine and technology, exam preparation (for German: Goethe, telc and ÖSD) and courses for companies.

Participants receive an Academy certificate for each completed level. Prices are available on request – [see all languages](/en/courses/language-courses) or [contact us](/en/contact) for the next start date.
