MASOK Academy's foreign language courses are open to anyone interested – including Danish, English, French, German, Italian, Russian, Spanish and Serbian for foreigners, with other languages on request. Lessons take place in small groups or one-to-one, with experienced instructors.

## Levels and formats

Courses start at beginner level (A1) according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), and levels are arranged for each language and group. A free placement test puts you in the right group. Formats include general language courses, professional language for medicine and technology, preparation for internationally recognized language exams, and courses for companies.

Participants receive an Academy certificate for each completed level. Prices are available on request – [see all languages](/en/courses/language-courses) or [contact us](/en/contact) for the next start date.
