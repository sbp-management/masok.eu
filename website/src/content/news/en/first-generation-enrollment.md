The first generation of students will begin their training at MASOK Academy this autumn. Applications are open for two programs: **Surgical Instrument Mechanic** and **Medical Administrative Assistant (MFA – Medizinische Fachangestellte)**.

## What the programs offer

Both programs last 9 months and follow the dual model: theoretical instruction at the Academy combined with practical training. Intensive German language training is part of every program, and graduates receive an internationally recognized AHK certificate.

## How to apply

Applying takes three steps:

1. An online application with your basic information and a CV
2. An online interview with the Academy team
3. Final selection and enrollment

You don't need to speak German before you enroll – language learning is part of the program. [See how to apply](/en/for-applicants).
